Photo Grid Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Photo Grid is an offline-first photo collage app. This policy describes the current version of the app.

Photos and projects

When you select photos, iOS gives Photo Grid access to the items you selected. Photo Grid copies those items into its private on-device storage so you can reopen your collage projects. Your photos and project data are not uploaded to a Photo Grid server. Projects and their copied photos remain on your device until you delete the project or remove the app. Device backups may include app data according to your iOS backup settings.

Saving and sharing

Photo Grid requests add-only Photos access when you choose to save an exported collage. It does not read your existing Photos library for this action. If you share a collage, iOS passes the image to the destination you select in the system share sheet. That destination's privacy practices apply.

Analytics and advertising

The current app build does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use your photos for advertising.

Children's privacy

Photo Grid does not collect personal information from users.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
