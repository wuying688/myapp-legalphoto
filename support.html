Photos and privacy

Photo Grid keeps selected photos and projects in the app's private storage on your device. Photos are not uploaded to a Photo Grid server. Saving an export requires the Photos permission requested by iOS. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and message to respond.

Common questions

Where are my projects stored?
Projects are stored locally in Photo Grid on the device where they were created.

How do I remove a project?
Open Your Projects, choose Manage, and delete the project. Its copied photos are removed from Photo Grid on that device.

How do I save a collage?
Open the project, choose Export, then choose Save image. iOS may ask permission to add the exported image to Photos.
