Photo Grid Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Photo Grid is an offline-first photo collage app for iPhone and iPad. This policy describes the app's current version.

Photos and projects processed on your device

You choose photos using Apple's system photo picker. iOS makes only the items you select available to Photo Grid. The app loads selected images on your device to show previews and, when you create a collage, copies them into Photo Grid's private app storage so you can reopen the project.

Projects may contain copies of your selected photos and the editing information needed to reopen the collage, such as its layout, crop and rotation settings, and captions. Photo Grid does not upload these photos or project details to servers operated by the developer.

Projects and their copied photos remain on your device until you delete the project in Photo Grid or remove the app. Your iPhone or iPad backup settings may include app data in an Apple or computer backup; backups are managed by the platform and your device settings.

Saving and sharing

Photo Grid asks for permission to add an image to Photos only after you choose Save image. The app uses add-only access for this action and does not use it to browse or read your existing Photos library.

If you choose Share, iOS presents its system share sheet and passes the exported collage to the destination you select. That app or service handles the image under its own privacy policy. Photo Grid does not receive information about what you do in the destination app.

Information sent to the developer; analytics and advertising

The current version has no account registration or online project service. It does not send your selected photos, collages, captions, or project data to the developer. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Photo Grid does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Photos and projects are processed and stored locally on the device, not sent to the developer. Parents and guardians can supervise photo selection and any sharing through the iOS share sheet.

Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact provided on the app's App Store product page.
